Android 16 (crDroid) for the **Huawei MateBook E Go** (Snapdragon 8cx Gen 3 /
SC8280XP) on a **mainline Linux kernel**, with hardware Vulkan on the Adreno 690.

This is the camera release. It also fixes the speaker gain that v0.5.0 shipped
clipping, replaces the mock thermal HAL with a real one, and closes a kernel
power-domain defect that had blocked cameras since the first attempt.

## Cameras

Both cameras now work in the camera app (Aperture ships with the ROM). As far
as we know this is the first time either camera on this machine has produced a
picture outside Windows.

**Front camera (Hynix hi846, 8 MP).** Preview and still capture work. The path
is mainline `camss` → libcamera's *simple* pipeline with the software ISP
(debayer, grey-world AWB, AGC) → libyuv → a small AIDL camera HAL written for
this port → the camera app. The sensor's analogue gain model was measured on
device (`gain = 1 + code/16`, verified to within 2.3 %), so exposure control
actually works; colour is white-balanced but not calibrated (there is no colour
matrix yet), so expect slightly flat colours.

**Rear camera — experimental.** The rear module on the unit this was developed
on is an **OmniVision OV13B10 (13 MP)**, not the Samsung S5K3L6 the device tree
had assumed for a year. We found that out by recovering the sensor's power
sequence from Huawei's Windows driver package, bringing the rails up, and
scanning the camera I²C bus — the sensor answered at address 0x36. With the
mainline `ov13b10` driver (plus a device-tree match and the board's power
sequence) the rear camera streams 4208×3120 frames and the camera app opens it.

Rear-camera caveats, all known:

* **No tuning yet.** libcamera has no gain model or tuning file for this sensor,
  so auto-exposure cannot raise gain; in dim light the preview is very dark.
  Daylight is fine. This is the next thing to fix.
* **No flash.** The flash LED hangs off the PMIC's flash controller, not a GPIO;
  it is not wired up yet.
* **Huawei shipped two rear modules for this board** (OV13B10 and S5K3L6). If
  your unit has the S5K3L6 variant, the rear camera will not probe — and because
  the mainline camera subsystem waits for every sensor in the device tree,
  **the front camera will disappear too**. If you install this build and have
  no cameras at all, that is why; please open an issue and say so, it tells us
  which module you have, and the fix is a device-tree switch.
* The rear camera's analogue supply is shared with the display's I/O rail. While
  the rear camera is powered the rail sits at 2.8 V instead of 1.8 V — this is
  exactly what Windows does on this board, and the panel is unaffected.

## The camera power-domain defect is fixed

Every earlier camera attempt worked once and then never again: the camera power
domain (`titan_top`) refused to power back on after its first collapse, and
only a reboot recovered it. After eight refuted hypotheses and a register-level
trace the cause turned out to be three clock generators in the SC8280XP camera
clock controller (`camnoc_axi`, `slow_ahb`, `fast_ahb`) that were not marked
*shared*. When the camera driver finished and released its clocks, the CAMNOC
bus clock was left pointing at a PLL that had just been switched off, and the
power-domain handshake with the bus never completed. Marking those generators
shared (they park on the always-on crystal when idle, as every sibling SoC's
driver already does) fixes it: the domain now powers up and down with normal
runtime PM, no boot-time workaround needed. The fix will be sent upstream.

## Speaker: the v0.5.0 gain staging was clipping

v0.5.0 shipped "digital +6 dB / PA −3 dB". Measured on device that produces
**−20 dB THD (about 10 % distortion)** on −6 dBFS material, because digital gain
sits ahead of the DAC and costs headroom. This build pins digital gain at unity
and takes the loudness from the power amplifier instead — about 20 dB cleaner
at the same volume.

## Thermal

The mock thermal HAL is gone. The real one reads the SoC's thermal zones. (The
mock reported a **36 °C** shutdown threshold; it never fired only because its
readings were constant. Had a real HAL been dropped in without changing the
threshold, the tablet would have powered off a few minutes after boot.)

## Smaller things

* The navigation bar is back.
* `wpa_cli` is in the image for Wi-Fi diagnostics.
* Kernel: ashmem and `xt_quota2` are back (a boot regression from a tree that
  was never committed), the camera GDSC wait values match the hardware reset
  values, and a genpd teardown bug that made re-binding the camera clock
  controller panic is fixed.
* Fixed a ueventd rule in the camera bring-up that had been overriding the
  GPU / sensor / video-codec device permissions.

## Known issues

* SELinux is still `permissive`.
* Audio (and Bluetooth) can deadlock after long uptime; a watchdog records the
  evidence under `/data/vendor/gaokun3/hangdump-*` — please attach it if you hit
  this.
* Internet downloads on the device are slow (1–2 MB/s) for reasons not yet
  established; LAN throughput is fine. In-system OTA is affected.
* Rear camera: see the caveats above.
